Import Korean Used Cars to Albania: Durrës RoRo Shipping Guide

If you buy cars for Albania, the Korean route is simple to plan: pick the car, confirm the FOB price, check the vehicle, pay, and ship by RoRo from Incheon to Durrës. This guide lists each step and what to confirm locally before you pay.

Why Albanian buyers look at Korea

Korean-origin cars have become a visible part of the Albanian market. A local news report (Euronews Albania, 15 April 2024) describes Korean cars arriving by RoRo ship at the new port of Porto Romano in Durrës, and quotes a dealer saying the cars are in better condition than comparable European cars and cheaper. That is one dealer's opinion, so compare real offers rather than relying on it: ask for several cars of the same model and year and check mileage, accident history and the inspection record on each.

Step 1: Choose the car and confirm the FOB price

Our price is FOB Incheon: the Korean listing price plus our domestic costs (car sale, delivery to port, deregistration, export clearance and loading). Sea freight, insurance, Albanian duty and taxes, port charges and registration are not included. Ask every seller which terms a quote uses before you compare two prices. See the cars in stock or send us a listing link.

Step 2: Check the car before you pay

Request the VIN, first registration date, mileage, full photos (interior, engine bay, tires, underbody) and the Korean inspection record. Read how to check a Korean used car before buying. An independent pre-purchase inspection is optional and charged at cost; you can see the report before the purchase goes ahead.

Step 3: Confirm Albanian import rules with your broker

Import rules (vehicle age, emission standard, documents, duty and taxes) are set by Albania and change over time. We do not quote a duty figure here. Ask your licensed customs broker for a written estimate for the exact car before you pay, and tell us anything the broker needs on the invoice.

Step 4: Pay, export and ship

After the contract is confirmed in writing, you pay by bank wire. We deregister the car for export and prepare the export documents (invoice, export certificate, bill of lading). Cars leave from Incheon by RoRo or container; see RoRo vs container shipping from Incheon. We confirm the next sailing and the destination port for each order.

Step 5: Clear customs and register in Albania

Your broker clears the car at the port, pays duty and taxes under the current rules, and arranges registration. Keep the commercial invoice, bill of lading and export documents together for this step.

Common mistakes

  • Comparing a FOB quote with a CIF quote.
  • Paying before the VIN and inspection record are checked.
  • Not getting a written duty and tax estimate from a local broker.
  • Choosing on price alone without checking mileage and accident history.

Frequently asked questions

How are Korean used cars shipped to Albania?

Most cars travel by RoRo (roll-on/roll-off) vessel from Incheon. Press coverage from April 2024 describes Korean cars arriving by RoRo at the new port of Porto Romano in Durrës. Containers are also possible; we confirm the option and the next sailing per order.

What does the FOB price include?

The FOB price is the car delivered to the Korean port, loaded, with export clearance. Sea freight, insurance, Albanian import duty and taxes, port charges and registration are extra.

What should I check before buying?

Ask your Albanian customs broker or the authority about the current rules for vehicle age, emissions and documents before paying for a car. Rules and rates change, so get the estimate in writing.

Can I buy without visiting Korea?

Yes. We send photos, the VIN, the Korean inspection record and a written contract before you pay. A pre-purchase inspection is optional and charged at cost.
