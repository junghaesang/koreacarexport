Export Hyundai Staria from Korea to Dubai

Looking for a Hyundai Staria in Dubai or the UAE? Here is what to check about Korean-spec vans, the versions available, current FOB prices from Incheon, and how the export works.

Why buyers in the Gulf look at Korean Staria vans

The Hyundai Staria is a large, modern van that is popular for staff transport, hotels, tourism, delivery and small business use. Buyers in Dubai and across the UAE often look to Korea because the supply of low-mileage used vans is large and the cars are generally well maintained. The cars on this page are Korean-market vehicles, so the specification is not the same as a GCC-specification car. Check this before you buy, because it affects registration, insurance, parts and resale.

Korean spec versus GCC spec

A car built for Korea can differ from a GCC-specification car in the air conditioning capacity, the emission setup, the infotainment language and some options. Korean vehicles are left-hand drive, which is the same steering side as in the UAE. Ask your local registration office, importer or customs broker whether a Korean-market Staria can be registered in your emirate and what inspection or modifications are required, because the rules change and we do not want you to rely on an outdated answer.

Staria Cargo, Lounge and passenger versions

The Staria comes as a passenger van with seven to eleven seats and as a Cargo van with a flat load area. The model name looks similar but the seats, engine and options differ, so always work from the stock number. Our current listings are Staria Cargo examples. If you need a passenger or Lounge version, tell us the number of seats, the engine and your budget on WhatsApp and we will search Korean listings for a match.
