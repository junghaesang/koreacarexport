Kia Carnival for Export from Korea

Buying a used Kia Carnival from Korea? Compare seat layouts and engines, see what to inspect, and get the FOB price from Incheon.

Why the Kia Carnival is a top export request

The Kia Carnival (sold as the Sedona in some markets) is a large family minivan with sliding doors and seven to eleven seats. Importers ask for it for family use, hotel and airport shuttles and tour companies. Korea has a steady supply of used Carnivals, and many are low in mileage.

Seven, nine or eleven seats

The seat layout changes the price and the way a country classifies the vehicle. A nine-seater is cheaper and often used as a shuttle; a seven-seater has more comfortable second-row seats. Some countries tax or register by seat count, so confirm the rules with a local broker before you choose.

Diesel, gasoline or hybrid

The Carnival has been sold with diesel and gasoline engines, and the newest generation also has a hybrid. Diesel is popular where fuel is cheap and where diesel quality is good. If your country has strict emission rules or poor diesel quality, a gasoline version can be the safer choice. Check the engine and emission standard in the listing, and ask us when it is not clear.

What to check on a used Carnival

Look at the sliding doors, the door seals and the rails, as these are used daily in shuttle service. Ask for photos of the rear seat rails, the cargo floor, the underbody and the dashboard odometer. Compare mileage with the year, and ask for the Korean inspection record. See how to check a Korean used car.

The price on this page is FOB Korea in US dollars. Sea freight, insurance and import costs in your country are separate; the Carnival is a large vehicle, so it is usually shipped by RoRo or in a container alone. See RoRo vs container and the container loading calculator.
