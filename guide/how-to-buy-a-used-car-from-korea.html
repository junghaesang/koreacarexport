How to Buy a Used Car from Korea: Step-by-Step Guide for Overseas Buyers

Buying a used car from Korea is straightforward once you know the order of steps: choose the car, confirm the FOB price, check the vehicle, pay, export, ship and clear customs at home.

Watch: how it works, door to port

Why buy from Korea

South Korea has a large domestic used-car market, and many cars are sold with low mileage and detailed service and accident records. Popular export models include the Hyundai Palisade, Kia Carnival, Hyundai Staria, Kia Sorento and Genesis GV80. Because the market is large, buyers abroad can usually compare several cars of the same model and year before deciding.

Step 1: Decide on model, budget and destination port

Start with three facts: the model and year range you want, your total budget, and the port or country you will import into. Your destination matters because import rules differ by country. Some countries restrict the age of imported vehicles or require right-hand or left-hand drive configurations. Ask your local customs broker or authority about these rules before you pay for a car.

Step 2: Understand the price terms

Export prices are normally quoted as FOB (Free on Board), meaning the price of the car delivered to the Korean port and loaded, with export clearance handled. It does not include sea freight, insurance, destination port charges, import duty or local registration. CFR adds sea freight to your port and CIF adds freight and insurance. Always ask which terms a quote uses before comparing two sellers.

Step 3: Pick a specific car

Ask the seller for the stock number, the VIN, the mileage, the year of first registration and a full set of photos, including the interior, engine bay, tires and underbody. A reputable seller should be able to provide the vehicle inspection record from Korea. See our guide on how to check a Korean used car before buying.

Step 4: Confirm the contract in writing

Before sending money, confirm in writing the car (stock number and VIN), the price and trade term, the payment method, who pays which costs, the expected shipping date and the documents you will receive. Keep the chat history and invoices.

Step 5: Pay and export

Payment is usually made by bank wire. After payment is confirmed, the car is deregistered for export in Korea and the export documents are prepared. The standard document set normally includes a commercial invoice, the export certificate and the bill of lading. Your customs broker will tell you exactly which documents your country requires.

Step 6: Shipping

Cars usually leave from Incheon. Shipping is by RoRo (roll-on/roll-off) vessel or in a container. Read our guide on RoRo vs container shipping from Incheon to choose the right option.

Step 7: Import clearance at home

When the vessel arrives, your broker clears the car with customs, pays import duty and taxes under your local rules, and arranges inspection and registration. Duty and tax rates vary widely and change over time, so get a written estimate from a licensed local broker instead of relying on a general figure.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Comparing a FOB quote with a CIF quote.
  • Paying before checking the VIN and inspection record.
  • Not checking your country's import rules for vehicle age, emissions and steering side.
  • Choosing a car only on price without checking mileage and accident history.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to buy a car from Korea?

The car price is quoted FOB. Sea freight, insurance, destination charges, duty, taxes and registration are extra and depend on the destination country. Ask for a quote that lists each cost separately.

Can I buy a car from Korea without visiting?

Yes. Most overseas buyers buy remotely, using photos, videos, the inspection record and a written contract. Request additional photos or a short video if anything is unclear.

How long does shipping take?

It depends on the destination and the vessel schedule. Ask for the next available sailing and the estimated transit time for your port; we confirm this per order.
