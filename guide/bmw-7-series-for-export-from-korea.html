How the FOB price is made

FOB Korea price is the Korean market price of the car plus the domestic costs, passed on at cost (inland transport, export clearance, deregistration, dealer handling and loading preparation), converted to US dollars at the day's rate. All other costs are charged at cost with receipts. See RoRo vs container shipping from Incheon.

FAQ

Are these prices final?

Prices are FOB Korea as of 2026-11-20. Shipping, insurance and import costs are separate, and a car can be sold at any time.

What is the Export Score?

A 100-point score from accident and insurance history, age, mileage, price against similar Korean listings, and export suitability. We list only cars scoring 80 (grade A) or higher.

How do I order a BMW 7 Series from Korea?
