    Common questions

    Korean used car export — FAQ

    What's the cheapest way to export a used car from Korea?
    We don't mark up the vehicle price. You pay close to the real Encar listing price — our margin comes from the 9.09% VAT refund on export, not from inflating the car's cost. You only pay the actual price, taxes, fees and freight to your port.
    Is JS INTERNATIONAL a trusted Korean used car export company?
    We are a buying agent, not a dealer — we have no stock of our own to push, so we can tell you when a car is not worth buying. Before we recommend anything we read its official inspection report and insurance history, and we publish real cars and real yard work on our YouTube and Instagram channels so you can see how we work before you pay anything.
    How does the Korean used car export process work?
    Find a car on Encar and send us the listing link on WhatsApp. We check the inspection report and quote the same day. Once you confirm the proforma invoice and pay, we handle purchase, shoring and shipping, and send you the ETD/ETA, loading photos, and final Bill of Lading.
    Can I export a used car from Korea to the UAE, Saudi Arabia or Central Asia?
    Yes. We regularly ship to the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Jordan, Iraq, Yemen, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Azerbaijan, Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan, door to port.
    Do I need to pay extra for vehicle inspection reports?
    No. Every car comes with the full Encar inspection report — accident history, panel and frame check — at no extra cost. If you'd like an independent third-party inspection as well, that's available for an optional $150 fee.
    Which documents do I receive?
    You receive the invoice (a proforma first), the Bill of Lading, the export declaration and the vehicle de-registration certificate, plus the loading photos. Some countries ask for extra papers — tell us your destination and we list what your customs will want.
    Can I import this car into my country?
    Rules differ by country — for example vehicle age limits, steering side, emission standards and engine-size taxes. Tell us your country on WhatsApp and we point out the usual restrictions before you choose a car. Always confirm the final rules with your own customs broker.
    How long does shipping take?
    To Dubai (Jebel Ali), about 3 weeks by container and 3–4 weeks by RoRo. Other destinations differ — ask us on WhatsApp for the current sailing schedule. Times vary with the vessel schedule.
